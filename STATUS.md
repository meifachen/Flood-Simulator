# STATUS: Flood Simulator

Updated 2026-11-10. Version 0.3.2: first real-site test (Winter Haven, FL; docs/sites/winter-haven-lake-martha.md) and a real-GPU benchmark, with two fixes they led to. Version 0.3.1 fixed the findings of the 0.3.0 review (docs/reviews/review-0.3.0-round1.md; response in docs/reviews/response-0.3.0-round1.md). Version 0.3.0 added per-inlet sizes, roughness and soils from land cover, velocity and hazard maps, and save and reopen results.

## What works
- **App:** offline single file `dist/FloodSimulator.html` (5.6 MB including three examples and SWMM 5.2.4 in WebAssembly).
- **Inputs:**
  - DEM GeoTIFF in any projected CRS from the PROJ list (5,252 EPSG codes);
  - building and level-boundary GeoJSON (reprojected from lon/lat or another EPSG);
  - SWMM5 `.inp`, and `.out` for one-way, in US or SI units.
- **SWMM coupling:**
  - One way: node flooding from the `.out` enters the 2D as point inflows.
  - Two way: EPA SWMM 5.2.4 runs in the page in step with the 2D. Node overflow goes onto the surface. Surface water returns through inlets (weir below the rim, orifice when surcharged, capped at half the cell's water per interval). `ALLOW_PONDING` is forced to NO, and the app says so.
  - Boundary levels: storage or outfall heads (from the `.out`, or live in two-way) or fixed levels.
- **Rain on the grid:** from a SWMM gauge (intensity, volume or cumulative) or uniform. A warning shows when it is combined with SWMM, since that counts runoff twice.
- **Engine:** CPU or WebGPU, identical arithmetic, with a water balance at every map. Stop works in about 0.2 s.
- **Results:**
  - map (hillshade offline, or OSM or USGS imagery online);
  - 3D view with buildings, water and SWMM nodes;
  - four charts and a water-balance table;
  - a two-way SWMM summary with the coupled `.rpt` and `.out` to download;
  - GeoTIFF (max depth, max water level, time of max), buildings CSV and SWMM exchange CSV.
- **Python tools:**
  - `prep_dem.py`: clip, reproject and resample, vertical unit factor, buildings, SWMM node check;
  - `make_synthetic.py` and `make_public_demo.py`.
- **Other tools:** `tools/build.py` builds the page, `tools/build_swmm_wasm.sh` builds SWMM in WebAssembly, and `tools/screenshot.py`.

- **New in 0.3.0:**
  - **Inlets:** a GeoJSON of inlets (point, node, weir length, grate area, coefficients, rim) replaces the single default size. Each inlet is its own sink; the half-the-water cap is shared among inlets in one cell. Junctions without a listed inlet use the default size or none (setting in the two-way box). An unknown node is reported and the inlet is ignored; an invalid size is refused.
  - **Land cover and soils:** an NLCD-coded GeoTIFF (any CRS; sampled at cell centres) sets Manning n and imperviousness per cell; a soils GeoJSON with a hydrologic soil group (dual groups use the last letter, D) sets Green-Ampt Ks and suction. Both tables are editable in the app. Unknown classes keep the global values and are reported.
  - **Velocity and hazard:** maximum speed and maximum depth × velocity, recorded where depth is at least 0.05 ft, on the CPU and on the GPU. New map views, legend classes, KPIs and popup values. The GeoTIFF export has 5 bands.
  - **Save and reopen:** `.fsim.gz` holds the grid, maps, maxima, SWMM summary and the layers on the map. A reopened run shows the map, 3D, charts and exports; Run is off until the inputs are loaded again.
  - **Closed edges:** an option to wall the grid edge; the outer ring of cells becomes a wall with no rain, so the balance stays closed.
  - Loading a new DEM clears the other layers and says so.

- **New in 0.3.2:**
  - `python/make_winter_haven_demo.py` builds a real-site example from public data: the City of Winter Haven storm sewer inventory, 3DEP lidar, NHD lakes, FEMA structures, NLCD, SSURGO and an Atlas 14 design storm (the data are downloaded, not stored in the repository).
  - Roof rain no longer goes into pockets of open ground closed in by buildings (a one-cell courtyard had collected 66 ft of water at 6 ft cells); the build reports such pockets.
  - The double-counting warning for rain on the grid shows only when the SWMM model has subcatchments that use that gauge.
- **Measured on a real GPU (Intel Core 7 150U laptop graphics, headless Chrome):** 12 to 32 times faster than the CPU on the synthetic example (6 ft: 3.4 s vs 110.1 s), same maps within 1.4e-5 ft. Winter Haven, 8 h two-way: 19.3 s at 12 ft, 84.6 s at 6 ft, 831 s at 3 ft (1.33 M cells).
- **Fixed in 0.3.1** (review IDs in brackets):
  - SWMM no longer keeps receiving the last inlet capture after the 2D run ends: the coupler zeroes the inlet inflow and then runs SWMM to its end [W9]. Test case: SWMM's external inflow 4,530 cf against 4,536 cf taken by the inlets (before: 104,326 cf extra in the reviewer's case).
  - A refused land cover, soils, inlets, buildings or boundary file leaves the layer loaded before in use, in the worker and on the page; its file box is cleared and the message says nothing was changed [W1, W2]. A DEM that fails to load changes nothing [AU-3]. A new DEM clears the worker's layers too [W5].
  - A listed inlet without `rim_ft` uses its node's SWMM rim as its crest, like a default inlet; a crest more than 0.5 ft from the cell ground is reported [C-F1]. The weir and orifice regimes meet at the crest, so the exchange no longer jumps when a flooding node sits at its rim: the US and SI models of the same site now send the same water to SWMM (37,267 cf each; before, SI sent 2.2 times as much) [C-S2].
  - Inlet checks: an inlet more than 300 ft from its node is reported; a junction whose listed inlets were all rejected gets no default inlet; zero, text and NaN sizes and a non-numeric `rim_ft` are refused at load, numbered as in the file; numeric node names and MultiPoint inlets are read; GeoJSON points without a `crs` member are read as longitude/latitude when they fit in degrees [C-F2, C-F3, C-F5, W3, W8].
  - Soils: the default IMD is now the Rawls drained deficit, porosity − field capacity: A 0.332, B 0.217, C 0.154, D 0.154 (was 0.3 for all). An IMD drier than porosity − wilting point is flagged [M-F1, AU-4].
  - Land cover: open water n 0.035 (Chow 1959) and cultivated crops n 0.06 (SWMM manual overland table, residue under 20%), each cited; the other 13 classes stay at Kalyanapu et al. (2009) Table 2 [M-F2].
  - Coverage: a land cover or soils layer that covers none or only part of the grid is reported, as are soil raster codes other than 1–4; misaligned arrays, infinite or blank table values and Ks over 50 in/hr are refused; `hsgCode` reads "1"–"4" and rejects fractions; rasters treat values beyond ±1e20 as NoData [W4, W6, M-F3, M-F4, M-F6, M-F7]. A cell size below the DEM resolution gives the cell-size message [W7].
  - Velocity: the time step now allows for the depth a point inflow or rain can add before the next reading, so a sudden inflow no longer stamps a start-up spike into the maxima (peak depth × velocity 1.000 ft²/s with 60 s and 5 s chunks; before 10.7 and 1.24) [E1]. Cells where the solver capped the flow at critical speed are recorded on the CPU and the GPU, hatched in the velocity views, counted in the KPI, explained in the legend and popup, and written as GeoTIFF band 6 [E2]. A hot start (`init` with `initVel`) carries the depth and velocity maxima together; `init` alone restarts both [E5]. The GPU ratio buffer can be reset without a WebGPU validation error [E6].
  - Saved results: they cannot be opened during a run; opening one puts the run's settings back in the panel; Run stays off until a DEM or example is loaded [AU-1, AU-2, AU-5]. The file now keeps NaN and Infinity, the critical-flow flags, and is refused when its header lists a negative, fractional or repeated array, a wrong type, a grid size that disagrees with itself, or fewer bytes than the file holds [R-F3, R-U1].
  - Smaller: loading an example clears the inlets file box; inlets show on the map before a .inp is loaded; the build message gives the land cover and soil cell counts [AU-6, AU-Q4, AU-Q5].

## Not tested
- Speed on a real GPU. Only SwiftShader was available; it is slower than the CPU here (CPU about 30–35× real time at 100,000–160,000 cells).
- The work PC's browser and its WebGPU policy.
- Online basemaps: tiles are blocked in the test sandbox, so only the hillshade fallback was tested.
- A DEM without an EPSG code (the EPSG entry box is coded but was not run).
- Grids near the 8-million-cell limit.
- Two-way coupling against another coupled model (e.g. PCSWMM 2D or HEC-RAS). It is verified for conservation and consistency only.
- A soils GeoTIFF in the browser (the code path exists; only soils GeoJSON was run in the app).
- Land cover classes other than NLCD codes (other schemes need an edited table).
- Results files from very large grids (file size and memory when reopening).
- The 0.3.1 fixes were checked by the tests above, not by a second independent review round.
- The work PC (only the home laptop's Intel GPU was tested).
- Winter Haven results against observed flooding or the City's own models; lakes held at 2018 lidar levels; inlet sizes not in the inventory (app defaults used).
- The browser checks were not run against 0.3.0 (its page lacks the new inputs), so their fail-first status is not established.

## Open items
- Inlet capture is capped at half the cell's water per coupling interval, so it shrinks with the cell size (Winter Haven: 488,918 cf at 12 ft, 217,612 at 6 ft, 72,909 at 3 ft) and sits below the inlet equations' own capacity (net drained 24% lower than uncapped at 12 ft). Planned fix: take what the inlet equations ask, limited each step by the water in the cell, and send SWMM the volume actually taken.
- Inlet clogging factors and inlet types (curb, grate, combination) with HEC-22 curves.
- Time series of velocity (only maxima are kept).
- Velocity and hazard checked against a benchmark (e.g. UK EA test 6A); only Manning flow is checked now.
