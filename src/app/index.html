2. Grid and surface

Land cover classes in this area: Manning n and impervious %. Starting values: NLCD n from Kalyanapu et al. (2009), except open water (11, Chow 1959) and cultivated crops (82, SWMM manual overland table), which that paper does not list; impervious % from the NLCD developed-class ranges. Check them for your site.

Green-Ampt by hydrologic soil group: Ks and suction from the Rawls et al. (1983) texture table; IMD is that table's porosity − field capacity (a drained soil). Dual groups such as B/D use D.

The values below apply where there is no land cover or soil data.

3. Water sources

SWMM already routes the runoff of this storm through its subcatchments. Rain on the grid as well counts that water twice where the grid overlaps the subcatchments.
